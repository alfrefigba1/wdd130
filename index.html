Nigeria

Nigeria is a large and diverse country located in West Africa and is the most populous nation on the continent. It is known for its rich cultural heritage, with more than 250 ethnic groups, including the Hausa, Yoruba, and Igbo peoples. Nigeria's capital is Abuja, while Lagos is its largest city and a major economic center. The country has abundant natural resources, particularly crude oil, and has one of Africa's largest economies. Nigeria is also famous for its vibrant music, film industry (Nollywood), delicious cuisine, and strong influence across Africa
